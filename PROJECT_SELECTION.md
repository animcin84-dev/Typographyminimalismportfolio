# Project selection — V6

## Flagship case studies
1. **HELIX** — strongest systems/reliability story; clear proof and technical depth.
2. **DALA2** — distinctive Kazakh-language AI research; reproducible evaluation story.
3. **JARVIS** — broad but coherent personal AI OS; strong interaction/design potential.
4. **GARGANTUA** — strongest immediate visual moment; real-time WebGL/GPU simulation.
5. **QADAM AI** — grounded social-impact AI with explicit privacy and anti-hallucination design.

## Secondary selected work
- **VTOR.AI** — credible Kazakhstan-wide civic/environment AI system; linked to public GitHub.
- **Robotics** — physical prototyping and field iteration; valuable range, but better as supporting work until stronger media exists.
- **HTML Challenge** — leadership/community signal; intentionally not competing with the technical flagship case studies.

## Intentionally omitted from the main portfolio
Other repositories and experiments are not bad; they are simply weaker for this particular narrative. A curated 5+3 structure is stronger than showing every repo.
